CHANGELOG

v1.1 (October 2026): a correction release, not a redesign. File names are the same as v1.0, so files can be swapped one for one.
- Arabic name: now "ايجيب جرين" (the letter ت is removed from the first word), to match the commercial register and the English spelling.
  The Arabic part of the English-first and Arabic-first lockups is redrawn in Readex Pro Arabic SemiBold, outlined, with the same
  two-tone split (ايجيب in Deep Green, جرين in Leaf Green) and the same ½ X gap. Each lockup is rebalanced: English-first stays flush
  left, Arabic-first stays flush right, and the clear-space and minimum-size rules still hold.
  Changed files: egypgreen-lockup-en-ar-*.svg/png and egypgreen-lockup-ar-en-*.svg/png.
  Unchanged: the wordmark, stacked logo, mark, app icon and favicon.
- The Arabic name is replaced on every page: the brand book, stationery, digital, catalog, labels, van, uniform and signage.
  The logo-misuse page now shows the misspelled Arabic form next to "EgyptGreen".
- Products: exactly three authorized-agent lines. Tormac stainless-steel submersible pumps (TS) and submersible motors (ECO),
  Astral uPVC bore-well column pipes, and LONGi solar panels. All other product types are removed from samples, the catalog cover,
  the WhatsApp reply, the vehicle and signage copy, and the icon set (now 10 icons; two icon files are removed).
  Catalog dividers are renumbered: 01 Submersible pumps & motors, 02 uPVC column pipes, 03 Solar panels.
- Submersible pumps replace surface pumps in every sample (spec page, pump label, tone of voice, type samples, posts).
  icon-pump.svg and the pump product drawing are redrawn as a submersible pump.
- "Every governorate" / "كل المحافظات" replaces any fixed count.
- Legal details are added to the invoice, quotation, letterhead footer and stamps: the legal name, CR 31614 (6th of October
  Investment Office) and the registered address. The Tax ID is still the placeholder "______".
- 07-Web: new egypgreen-tokens.css file.
- Sample values (models, prices, specs) are examples only: replace them with the manufacturers' data.
