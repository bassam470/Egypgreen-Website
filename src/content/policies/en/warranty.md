Every product we distribute is genuine factory stock, sold with authorized-agent warranty under each manufacturer's warranty terms.

## Warranty period

We state the warranty period and terms for each product on the quote and the invoice. For LONGi panels, the manufacturer publishes the product and performance warranty for each model in its datasheet.

## What the warranty covers

Manufacturing and material defects that appear under normal operating conditions, under the manufacturer's terms.

## What it doesn't cover

- Installation or operation contrary to the manufacturer's instructions.
- Operation on a voltage outside the allowed range, phase loss, or without suitable electrical protection.
- Dry running, sand above the allowed limit, or cooling flow below the required velocity.
- Breakage or damage from transport, storage or handling after delivery.
- Opening or repair by an unauthorized party.
- Normal wear of parts.

## How to make a claim

1. Contact us on WhatsApp or by phone.
2. Send a photo of the invoice, the product's serial number if it has one, and a description of the problem and operating conditions.
3. Our team reviews the claim and may ask to inspect the product to confirm the cause.
4. If the fault is covered, it is repaired or replaced under the manufacturer's terms.
