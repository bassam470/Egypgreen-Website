This policy explains how egypgreen.com, owned by EgypGreen (ايجيب جرين لصناعة وتجارة الطلمبات ش.ذ.م.م), handles visitor data.

## What we don't collect

- The site has no account sign-up and no forms that send data to our servers.
- We don't show prices or take any payments on the site.
- We don't currently use analytics, advertising tools or tracking cookies.

## The quote cart

The products you add to the cart, and the details you type on the quote page (name, governorate, city and notes), are stored only in your browser using local storage (localStorage). They reach us only when you press "Send request on WhatsApp" and send the message yourself from the WhatsApp app. You can delete them at any time by emptying the cart or clearing the site's data in your browser.

## WhatsApp and phone contact

When you message us on WhatsApp or call us, we use your number and what you send only to answer your request, prepare a quote and arrange delivery. We don't sell your data or share it with third parties for marketing. WhatsApp messages are also subject to WhatsApp's privacy policy.

## Google Maps

The map on the Contact page loads only when you press "Show the map". Once loaded, Google handles that request under its own privacy policy.

## Future changes

If we later add analytics or advertising tools, we will update this page before switching them on and explain what data they collect.

## Contact

For any question about this policy, contact us on the numbers listed on the Contact page.
